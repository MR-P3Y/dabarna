Guide
How do bank card deposits work?

Enter an amount in toman, choose and copy the destination card, then submit a clear receipt from your gallery or files.

  • The receipt amount must match the submitted amount.
  • The tracking number or payment time must be visible on the receipt.
  • Funds are added to your wallet after admin review.

How do crypto payments work?

Enter an amount in toman and choose a network to create an invoice at the current rate. Pay with a connected wallet or use the QR code or copied address.

  • Your wallet network must exactly match the invoice network.
  • Pay the exact amount displayed.
  • Network fees are separate and calculated by your wallet.
Secure wallet connection Toofan Dabarna cannot access your password, recovery phrase or private key. Payment requires your final confirmation inside your wallet.
